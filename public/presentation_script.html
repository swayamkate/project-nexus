Speaker 1 • Avishkar Kedar (Lead)
0:00 – 2:00 (2 Mins)

"Good morning, respected judges and everyone present here. We are Team CareerLoop Innovators, and my name is Avishkar. Today, our team is going to present a solution to one of the biggest blind spots in our country's skill development ecosystem: the complete lack of long-term tracking after a student finishes a vocational course.

Let us understand this problem through a scenario that happens every single day right inside our training institutes and colleges. Imagine a vocational computer science trainee named Rahul. Rahul enrolls in a specialized government-sponsored training program to learn modern web development. Right now, the existing government portals are very good at capturing his initial data: they record the day Rahul registers, they track his daily biometric attendance, they store his marks in internal assessments, and finally, they record the day he receives his completion certificate. At this exact moment, the traditional system logs a green tick and declares, 'Mission accomplished, Rahul is successfully trained.'

But let us pause right there and ask the real question: does this piece of paper guarantee that Rahul actually secured a job? Does it tell us if he is using those web development skills in a real company, or if he is stuck doing something completely unrelated? Does it tell us if his salary is growing, or if he was laid off after just two months? The hard truth is that right now, this critical post-graduation data is completely missing.

Why does this data disappear? Because the moment students graduate, reality sets in: candidates change their phone numbers, relocate to different cities for job hunting, or update their emails. At the same time, busy employers do not have the time to constantly log into multiple government portals to report whether Rahul is still working there or how he is performing. Furthermore, different skilling schemes operate in complete silos with incompatible IDs and disconnected databases.

Because of this massive data gap, policymakers and government authorities are flying blind. They cannot determine which training centers are actually building great software engineers and which ones are just printing empty certificates. They cannot see the actual skill gaps between what is taught and what industry demands, making it almost impossible to allocate public funds with accountability.

To fix this, we don't just need another database; we need a continuous tracking ecosystem that is credible, respects student privacy, and works automatically without burning out administrative staff. To show you how we engineered this, I will now hand over the mic to my teammate."

Speaker 2 • The Solution & Student Journey
2:00 – 4:30 (2.5 Mins)

"Thank you, Avishkar. My name is [Teammate Name], and I am going to walk you through our proposed solution. To solve the exact problem Rahul faces, our team has designed the CareerLoop Longitudinal Skilling Outcome and Impact Platform. When we use the word 'longitudinal,' it simply means we don't just stop tracking on graduation day; we follow the student's career journey across months and years.

Our core model completely shifts the focus away from just counting how many certificates were printed, and instead measures actual, real-world employment and wage outcomes.

Let us trace Rahul's journey step-by-step through our platform to see how this works in practice:

• Step 1: Trainee Registration. When Rahul joins, our system creates a secure, unique trainee profile. Crucially, this is completely consent-based with masked Aadhaar safeguards, giving Rahul full ownership of his privacy.

• Step 2: Training and Assessment. As Rahul completes his technical modules—like web development, databases, or cloud infrastructure—his course progress and regular assessment scores are automatically mapped to his profile.

• Step 3: Certification. Once he finishes, his final state-certified credential is attached to his digital profile with an immutable, cryptographically signed QR code.

• Step 4: Placement or Self-Employment. When Rahul is hired by a tech startup or registers a freelancing micro-enterprise under Udyam, CareerLoop captures those wage or business turnover details right away.

• Step 5: Employer & Admin Verification. To prevent fake entries, district evaluators and partner employers verify Rahul's role, salary, and PF/ESIC UAN numbers with one click.

• Step 6: Longitudinal Follow-ups. This is where our platform shines. Instead of forgetting Rahul, our system automatically triggers check-ins at the 3-month, 6-month, 12-month, and 24-month marks. We verify job retention, wage trajectory, and whether his training actually matched the skills he needs on the job.

• Steps 7 & 8: Analytics and Decision Making. All this verified data feeds directly into high-level dashboards, empowering authorities to pinpoint course effectiveness and demographic trends.

Now, my teammate will explain the modern engineering and smart automation that powers this entire loop."

Speaker 3 • Tech Stack & Smart Automation
4:30 – 6:30 (2 Mins)

"Thank you, [Teammate Name]. My name is [Teammate Name], and I am going to talk about the engineering and technology behind CareerLoop. When we designed this system, we knew that if we relied on manual phone calls or paper forms, the platform would collapse under scale. We needed smart, frictionless automation.

Let’s look at how our tech stack is divided:

• The Frontend Interface: Built with Next.js 16 and React 19 for lightning-fast sub-second transitions. We created distinct, role-based interfaces with multi-lingual support (English, Marathi, Hindi) and system-wide dark/light theme switching.

• The Data and Security Layer: User profiles, course catalogs, and longitudinal wage records are securely stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL 17 database. We implemented Row Level Security (RLS) and cryptographic JWT sessions, ensuring a trainee can only access their own dossier while administrators govern aggregated district statistics.

• The Automation Engine: How do we conduct 3, 6, 12, and 24-month check-ins without calling thousands of candidates manually? We engineered automated webhook cron routes (/api/cron/milestones) integrated with the Telegram Bot API & SMS Gateway. When Rahul hits his 6-month employment milestone, our worker fires an interactive survey right to his Telegram app. He taps two buttons to report his job retention and wage progression in under ten seconds without downloading extra apps.

• Data Telemetry: Using Recharts, raw milestone submissions are instantly converted into interactive wage growth curves, district employment heatmaps, and retention funnel charts for executive decision makers.

Our architecture is deployed on Cloudflare and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. I will now invite our final teammate to discuss our outcome loop and national impact."

Judge Defense Matrix • Top Tough Questions & Answers
Q1: How do you prevent candidates from entering fake salary or job details?
Answer: CareerLoop employs a 3-tier validation mechanism: (1) Mandatory PF/ESIC UAN or Udyam number validation, (2) Direct salary slip/offer letter document upload to our encrypted vault, and (3) 1-click administrative verification where district evaluators cross-check and stamp the record as verified. Unverified records are clearly flagged and excluded from official DBT disbursements.
Q2: What if a trainee changes their phone number or ignores follow-up surveys?
Answer: We solve this via multi-channel fallback: automated Telegram bot interactive buttons, secondary WhatsApp messages, and email magic links. Furthermore, trainees are incentivized to keep their profile active through access to accredited NPTEL courses, government credit schemes (CMEGP/Mudra), and free micro-enterprise digital invoicing tools.
Q3: How does CareerLoop comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023?
Answer: CareerLoop implements Zero-PII masking: Aadhaar numbers are cryptographically masked (`XXXX-XXXX-1234`), all follow-up data collection is strictly consent-driven, and candidate data is isolated using PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS). Trainees have full rights to export or request data purging anytime.
Q4: How does this system scale to handle 500,000 trainees across Maharashtra?
Answer: The architecture uses serverless Next.js edge handlers combined with Supabase PostgreSQL connection pooling and Redis-compatible asynchronous webhook workers for batch survey dispatching, capable of handling 50,000+ survey dispatches per minute without database lock contention.
